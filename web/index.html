Printing

How to print

Settings for an FDM printer. Pass them to the print shop together with the files.

Fit test first

Print chabad_fit_test on the same printer in both plastics. The plugs should go in firmly, without play.

PETG + heat-resistant

Stem and inner branch halves in PETG; the parts with cups in ASA, ABS or PC — they sit next to the flames.

Bed from 256 mm

Branch pieces are at most 211 mm. The 272 mm rods fit diagonally on a 256 mm bed.

Walls and infill

Stem: 4 perimeters, gyroid 12–15 %. Branches: 4–5 perimeters, gyroid 25–30 %. Pins and rods: solid.

Fine layers

0.12–0.16 mm layers keep the wreaths, leaves and flame veins crisp.

Supports

Branches print lying down with supports; the stem pieces stand upright with a brim.

Assembly

Assembly in four steps

1

Stem

Join the foot, the lower stem, the upper stem and the shamash cup on their pins and glue the joints.

2

Branches

Insert the branches from the stem outwards: 1, the inner halves of 2–4, then the outer halves with the cups. Glue every joint.

3

Stiffening rod

Slide the two rod halves through the top knops under the cups from both sides until the joint inside the stem snaps.

4

Glue

Put a drop of epoxy or CA glue into every rod hole in the top knops from the back.

Safety

Use tea lights only in their aluminium cups and never leave burning candles unattended. Plastic must not touch the flame.
